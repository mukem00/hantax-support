Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to play?

No account or sign-in is required.

How do I unlock new levels?

Continue progressing through the available levels to unlock the next stages.

Where can I change game settings?

Open the Settings (Ayarlar) screen to adjust music, sound effects, vibration, difficulty and available language options.

How do I report a crash or gameplay problem?

Contact support using the email above, with details on how to reproduce the issue if possible.
